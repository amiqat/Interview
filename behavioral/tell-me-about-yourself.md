## Tell Me About Yourself

**Type:** Self-Assessment

**Topic:** Introduction, Background

### Question

"Tell me about yourself" or "Walk me through your resume"

### Purpose

This question helps interviewers:
- Understand your background and experience
- Assess communication skills
- Gauge your ability to prioritize information
- Set the tone for the interview

### How to Answer

**Structure (The "Present-Past-Future" Framework):**

1. **Present** (30-40 seconds)
   - Current role and responsibilities
   - Key achievements or projects
   - Relevant skills and expertise

2. **Past** (30-40 seconds)
   - Educational background
   - Previous relevant experiences
   - Key career transitions and why

3. **Future** (20-30 seconds)
   - Why you're interested in this role
   - What you're looking for in your next position
   - How this aligns with your career goals

### Example Answer (Software Engineer)

> "I'm currently a Senior Software Engineer at TechCorp where I lead the backend development for our e-commerce platform. Over the past two years, I've architected and implemented microservices that handle over 1 million transactions daily, improving our system's reliability from 99.5% to 99.9%. I work primarily with Java, Spring Boot, and AWS, and I mentor a team of three junior engineers.
>
> Before this role, I graduated from State University with a degree in Computer Science, where I developed a strong foundation in algorithms and distributed systems. I started my career at StartupX, where I was one of the first engineers and got exposure to the full development lifecycle – from architecting solutions to deploying to production.
>
> I'm now looking for an opportunity where I can have a broader impact on system architecture and contribute to building products used by millions of users. When I learned about your company's mission to revolutionize online education and your technical challenges around scaling to support global reach, I knew this would be the perfect next step in my career."

### Tips

**Do:**
- Keep it to 2-3 minutes maximum
- Focus on professional experience relevant to the role
- Use specific examples and metrics when possible
- Show enthusiasm and confidence
- Practice beforehand to sound natural

**Don't:**
- Give your entire life story
- Ramble or go off on tangents
- Focus too much on personal life
- Speak negatively about current/past employers
- Memorize a script word-for-word (be natural)

### Framework Variations

**STAR Method Elements:**
- **Situation:** Your current/past context
- **Task:** Your responsibilities
- **Action:** What you did
- **Result:** Achievements and outcomes

**For Career Changers:**
- Emphasize transferable skills
- Explain your motivation for the change
- Show continuous learning and preparation

**For New Graduates:**
- Focus on education and projects
- Highlight internships or relevant experience
- Show enthusiasm and willingness to learn

### Follow-up Questions

After this answer, interviewers might ask:
- "Tell me more about [specific project mentioned]"
- "What made you decide to leave [previous company]?"
- "What interests you most about this role?"

### Common Mistakes

1. **Too long or too detailed** - Losing interviewer's attention
2. **Too generic** - Not tailoring to the specific role
3. **Chronological resume recitation** - Boring and unhelpful
4. **Negative tone** - Complaining about past roles
5. **Lack of preparation** - Seems disinterested or unprofessional
